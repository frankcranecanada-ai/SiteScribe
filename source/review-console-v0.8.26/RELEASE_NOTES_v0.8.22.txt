SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.22

- Report record picker now follows B05 primary/secondary/activity order. Activities stay nested beneath their secondary instead of being scattered by work date.
- A secondary excluded by filters is displayed as non-selectable parent context if one of its activities qualifies. Eligible keys, date/status/category filters, group selection, report output ordering, billing rules, direct hours and Excel inputs are unchanged.
- Added indentation/tree lines and italic activity descriptions to the picker. Long descriptions wrap.
- Reports now locks background scrolling and uses one vertical overlay scroll at all screen sizes, including landscape phones. Wide report tables retain horizontal scrolling. Screen-only rules preserve print layout.
- B05 secondary titles with activities use light-bold weight 600; childless secondaries remain unchanged. Activity descriptions stay italic.

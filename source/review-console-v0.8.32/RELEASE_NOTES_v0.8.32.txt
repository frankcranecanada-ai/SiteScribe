SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.32 - Hours Report dates and print layout

Candidate continuing v0.8.31 at the user's request. Not an accepted baseline.

- From and To dates are grouped in the report controls. Opening Reports starts
  with the first and last days of the current local calendar month. Custom
  dates remain while the panel is open and reset when it is reopened.
- The Hours Report header is compact plain metadata without boxed pills.
- Total Hours appears in the Hours Detail table directly after the final
  selected record, instead of a detached block. The header summary lists
  record and time-entry counts without repeating the total.
- Printed Hours Report status uses St, O and C with an explicit legend. All
  nine table columns fit a portrait Letter page and long activity text wraps.
  Full Open and Closed words remain on screen.
- The report continues to include only positive direct-hour entries; stored
  records, the MDS report and Hours Excel content are unchanged.
- index.html updates the visible version and cache markers to v0.8.32.

See VERIFICATION_v0.8.32.txt for checks and limits.

SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.31 - Hours Report layout and billable rows

Candidate continuing v0.8.30 at the user's request. Not an accepted baseline.

- The on-screen Hours Detail table now has nine usable columns. Activity and
  other long descriptions wrap inside their cells, and Status retains width.
  Narrow screens keep horizontal table swipe; printed cells also wrap.
- Hours Report selection, on-screen rows, summary and Excel export include
  only secondary/activity entries with more than 0 direct hours. A positive
  activity remains eligible even when its parent secondary has zero hours.
  When no positive-hour entries remain, the report shows an empty message.
- Other report types retain their existing row selection. Stored hours and
  calculated parent totals are unchanged. No Dropbox, email or site data
  actions were added.
- index.html updates the visible version and cache markers to v0.8.31.

Verification: regression_check_v0.8.31.js, browser_check_v0.8.31.cjs,
regression_check_v0.8.14.js and inherited responsive browser checks.
Tests use synthetic local data. Physical iPhone acceptance remains pending.

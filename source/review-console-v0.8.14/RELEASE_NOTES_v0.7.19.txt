SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.19
Report Date Ordering

What changed
- Added a Date Order field to the Reports panel with Report default, Oldest to newest, and Newest to oldest choices.
- Primary records are ordered by Record Date, whose authoritative basis is Created Date with the existing legacy fallbacks.
- Secondary records are ordered by Work Date when entered in the Hours section; Created Date remains the fallback for secondary records without a Work Date.
- Daily Site, Hours, and MDS Billing reports default to oldest-to-newest.
- Open Items retains urgency priority by default. An explicit oldest/newest choice orders its records by their record/work dates while preserving the report's urgency sections.
- Communication Activity defaults to newest-to-oldest by its activity timestamp.
- The report header states the effective ordering and date basis.
- Record selection lists, on-screen reports, printed reports, copied report text, Hours Excel rows, and MDS Excel summary rows use the selected ordering.
- MDS weekly tabs follow the selected week direction while preserving the required Saturday-Friday layout within each timesheet.

Data integrity
- No record schema change was made.
- No stored Created Date, Work Date, sourceText, status, deliverable, billing, or contact data is changed by sorting.
- Sorting operates on copied arrays and does not reorder Working Memory.
- Dropbox, Parser, email, import/export, and autosave behavior are unchanged.

Date rules
- Primary: createdAt; legacy fallback updatedAt, sourceDate, sourceLogDate, then site shift date.
- Secondary: workDate; fallback createdAt, updatedAt, then the parent primary record date.
- Communication Activity: activity timestamp.
- Open Items default: deliverable urgency, then deliverable date.

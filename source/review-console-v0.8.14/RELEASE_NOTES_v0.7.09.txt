SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.09 — Site-Scoped Contact Copy

Change:
- A new contact card now uses two selections: Source site, then Contact from selected site.
- The contact list contains only people stored in the chosen saved site and shows email, phone, and role where available.
- The current site cannot be selected as a source.
- Selecting a person copies name, email, phone, role, aliases, and active status into the new contact card.

Preserved:
- Each saved site remains self-contained.
- No shared people directory, automatic merge, move, or cross-site update is created.
- All Site Contacts remains a read-only lookup.
- Contacts / Departments routing, site title-bar reorder, iPhone drag lock, records, Dropbox, reports, and autosave are unchanged.

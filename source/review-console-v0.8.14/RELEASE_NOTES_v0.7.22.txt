SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.22
Primary-Screen Secondary Date

Change
- Inline secondary records on the Working Memory / primary screen now show a short date in their right-hand rail.
- The date is Work Date when saved; otherwise it is that secondary record's Created Date.
- The display is date-only (for example, Aug 12), so the row keeps as much title space as possible.

Data protection
- Display-only change. No record dates, JSON files, hours, billing, reports, or autosave data are changed.

SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.14 — Email Activity Record Detail

- Email Activity entries are now collapsed, expandable records.
- The collapsed line shows subject, timestamp, activity/status, and recipient.
- Expanding an entry shows the linked primary record, any originating secondary record, routing intent, and a readable preview of the draft email text.
- New email activity preserves a snapshot of the record descriptions and draft text so history remains understandable even after later record edits.
- Existing activity entries remain compatible: where a linked record/draft still exists, SiteScribe fills in the available context when shown.
- No automatic email sending, contact routing, record status, Dropbox, or report-selection behaviour changed.

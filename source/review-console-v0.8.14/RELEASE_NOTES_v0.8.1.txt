SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.1
B05 Branch Click Repair

Purpose
- Correct the B05 click mapping following operator feedback.

Changes
- Clicking a primary record body now shows or hides its secondary records.
- Clicking a secondary record body now shows or hides its activity records.
- The existing primary OPEN pill still opens primary detail.
- The secondary OPEN status pill is now an explicit control that opens secondary detail.
- Activity records continue to open their detail because they are the capped third level and have no children.
- Branch arrows/counts remain explicit show/hide controls.

Protected behavior retained
- Capped primary > secondary > activity model; no fourth level.
- Direct activity reporting and parent-context exports.
- sourceText, protected import choices, closed-record locks, Dropbox safeguards, parser protections, draft-only communications, and no credentials.

Candidate status
- Test candidate only. v0.7.33 remains the accepted baseline; this does not become accepted until user testing and approval.

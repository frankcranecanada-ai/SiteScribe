SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.7
Closed Secondary Recovery Repair

Change
- A closed secondary record under an open primary now displays Reopen and Delete controls when opened.
- Its editor remains locked until it is reopened.
- Delete remains restricted to closed secondary records and continues to require confirmation.

Protected behavior retained
- Closed primary and closed-parent activity locks remain intact.
- No automatic delete, status change, report, parser, Dropbox, or communication behavior is added.

Candidate status
- Test candidate only. It is not an accepted baseline until user testing and explicit approval.

SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.28 - Mobile editor and touch access

Candidate continuing v0.8.27 at the user's request. Not a newly accepted baseline.

- Expense controls occupy a separate, bounded grid row. Selecting Expense no
  longer widens the secondary/activity editor or pushes Save/Add Activity off
  the screen in landscape. Applies to all three detail-entry contexts.
- Touch B05 OPEN/COPY hit boxes are at least 44 x 36 CSS px while their oval
  faces remain 22 px high. Six-dot grips are 28 x 32; branch controls are
  32 x 32. Glyphs and lettering retain their compact appearance. Actual boxes
  reserve space, rather than overlapping neighbouring controls invisibly.
- Touch configuration buttons and saved-site/Dropbox row buttons receive
  32 px minimum heights. Report filter labels remain fully clickable, with
  additional vertical touch room. Desktop touch-target styling is unchanged.
- At touch viewport widths up to 1024 px, primary, secondary and activity
  detail use the full screen. A sticky header keeps Back accessible while
  scrolling. Desktop retains the established popup and Close label.
- Mobile detail permits both vertical scrolling and browser pinch zoom.
  Back invokes the existing explicit-close/commit handler. No browser history
  or automatic sending/deletion behaviour was added.
- Non-interactive toast notifications are click-through. Even the empty hidden
  toast box previously intercepted an OPEN pill in some scrolled positions.
- App/data/storage/import/Dropbox/report/PDF/drag handlers are unchanged.
  app.js differs from v0.8.27 only in its two release-version strings.

Verification: regression_check_v0.8.28.js and browser_check_v0.8.28.cjs.
Physical iPhone Safari pinch, keyboard and native drag still require user
confirmation. Do not confuse emulation success with physical-device acceptance.

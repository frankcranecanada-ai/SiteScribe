SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.28
B05 Primary and Secondary Record Drag Grips

Change
- Each B05 primary and secondary record now has a six-dot grip at its left edge.
- The grip is the deliberate place to start a drag for reordering, moving under another primary, promoting a secondary, or blending records.
- On desktop, a movable grip becomes clear on row hover. On touch/coarse-pointer devices it stays lightly visible.
- The grip carries the tooltip: Drag to reorder or move. Locked records display a disabled grip with an explanatory tooltip.
- Valid move-under destinations retain their dashed highlight. Valid before/after reordering positions use a clear blue insertion line.

Scope protection
- Clicking a primary or secondary row retains its existing open/expand behavior. Clicking the grip does not open the record.
- Closed and deliverable-locked movement rules remain unchanged.
- No record data, date, status, billing, reporting, autosave, Dropbox, Parser, or saved-site behavior is changed.

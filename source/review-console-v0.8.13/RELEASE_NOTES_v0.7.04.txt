SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.04 — Contact Dropdown Fix

Fixes the new-contact “Prefill from All Site Contacts” picker.

- Clicking its chevron now opens and keeps the native contact list open.
- The list remains available until a contact is selected or the user dismisses it.
- Selecting a person still copies the saved name, email, phone, role, aliases, and active status into the new contact card only.
- Add Contact and Remove actions remain unchanged.

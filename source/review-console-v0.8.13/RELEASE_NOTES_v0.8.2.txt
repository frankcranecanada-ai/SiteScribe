SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.2
Inline Activity Entry

Changes
- Opening a secondary record now keeps its editor in place and shows an Add Activity Record section beneath it.
- The activity entry has its own text box plus Start, End, Hours, Work Date, Billing, Expense, Currency, Expense Type, and Deliverable controls.
- Add Activity saves the new third-level record directly under the open secondary and leaves the operator on that secondary screen, ready for the next activity.
- The prior Add Activity navigation pill is removed.

Protected behavior retained
- Three-level cap: Primary > Secondary > Activity only.
- Existing activity detail editing, closed-record locks, reporting, sourceText, import protections, Dropbox protections, and draft-only communications.

Candidate status
- Test candidate only. It is not an accepted baseline until user testing and explicit approval.

SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.29
IPhone B05 Drag Promotion Highlight Repair

Change
- The full Working Memory dashed promotion box no longer appears merely when an iPhone user selects a secondary record's six-dot grip.
- Promotion feedback appears only after the dragged secondary is deliberately moved into a narrow primary-alignment zone to the left of its normal secondary position.
- iPhone drag events that initially report a horizontal coordinate of zero cannot activate promotion feedback.

Scope protection
- The primary/secondary six-dot grips, row opening, reordering, moving under another primary, and deliberate secondary promotion all remain available.
- Existing dashed valid-destination and before/after insertion feedback remains unchanged.
- No record data, dates, statuses, billing, reports, autosave, Dropbox, Parser, or saved-site behavior is changed.

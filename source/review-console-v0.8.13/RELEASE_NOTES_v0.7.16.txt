SiteScribe Review Console v0.7.16
MDS Billing — Exclude Closed Records

- The MDS Billing selector, on-screen report, totals, and Excel export now omit a billing line when its secondary record is Closed.
- Billing lines under a Closed primary record are also omitted.
- Open primary and secondary billing records continue to support Reg, OT, and Expense entries.
- The normal Hours, Daily Site, Open Items, and Communication Activity reports are unchanged.

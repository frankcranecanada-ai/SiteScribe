SiteScribe Review Console v0.8.8

Purpose
- Repair recovery of a closed secondary or activity record without weakening record-lock rules.

Change
- The locked editor remains unavailable for edits.
- When the selected closed record's parent remains open, its Reopen and Delete controls now render in a separate recovery footer below the lock notice.
- The lock notice now identifies whether the primary, parent secondary, or selected record must be reopened.

Protections retained
- A closed primary still prevents recovery controls for descendants.
- A closed secondary still prevents recovery controls for its activities until the secondary is reopened.
- Delete remains restricted to closed records and requires its existing confirmation.
- No automatic actions, date changes, status changes, or communications were added.
